Wash order

Four steps, in the right order

Skipping a step is the fastest way to swirl paint you just corrected.

01

Pre-wash

Toxic Foam breaks up grime before a towel ever touches the panel, cutting swirl risk dramatically.

02

Wheels & contact wash

Wheel Venom first, then a two-bucket contact wash with a dedicated wheel-only mitt.

03

Dry & decontaminate

A clean microfiber pass, then a clay bar or iron remover pass if the paint still feels rough.

04

Protect

9H Ceramic Shield goes on last, on fully clean and dry paint — never over old wax.
